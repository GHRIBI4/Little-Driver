================================================================================
          UNITY 4-WAY INTERSECTION TRAFFIC SIMULATION ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
This project simulates a 4-way urban intersection managed by a Finite State 
Machine (FSM) controlling traffic lights (Scenario 1), while individual cars 
autonomously navigate the environment using Behavior Designer behavior trees, 
Unity NavMesh paths, and centralized intersection arbitration.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CORE COMPONENTS & RESPONSIBILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. TrafficLightFSM.cs (The Signal Brain)
   - Governs the 5-phase traffic light cycle (NS Green -> NS Yellow -> EW Green -> 
     EW Yellow -> All Red) based on configurable timers.
   - Updates physical TrafficLight 3D models and logs phase transitions to the console.
   - Exposes GetLight(Direction approach) for query-based state reading.

B. TrafficLight.cs (Physical Lamps)
   - Controls the emission and color properties of individual red, yellow, and 
     green lamps on the 3D traffic light asset.

C. IntersectionManager.cs (The Central Arbitrator)
   - Acts as the single source of truth for intersection rules and spacing.
   - Manages the center lock system to coordinate opposing left-turners safely.
   - Evaluates oncoming traffic conflicts and queue leadership.
   - Dynamically builds Bezier curve waypoints for turns (Straight, Left, Right).

D. TrafficCar.cs & TrafficTasks.cs (The Vehicle AI & Behavior Tree)
   - Evaluates whether to stop for red/yellow lights or yield to oncoming traffic.
   - Implements "Solution 2" (Waiting Pocket): Left-turners pull forward and shift 
     left on a green light when blocked by oncoming traffic, allowing Straight 
     and Right cars to bypass them without getting stuck.
   - Includes a deadlock watchdog timer to automatically un-stick vehicles 
     inside the intersection.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW THE SYSTEMS LINK TOGETHER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Signal Synchronization:
   - TrafficLightFSM manages the active state of each approach. When a car 
     approaches the intersection, its Behavior Tree evaluates the conditional 
     task MustWaitAtStopLine, which queries TrafficCar -> IntersectionManager -> 
     TrafficLightFSM to check if the current light is Green.

2. Decentralized Decision Making, Centralized Rules:
   - Individual cars make no global decisions; their Behavior Trees execute 
     actions like HoldAtStopLine, DriveRoute, and ManageCenterLock. 
   - Whenever a car needs rule-based verification (e.g., checking oncoming traffic 
     conflicts or claiming the intersection center), it calls methods on the 
     shared IntersectionManager instance.

3. Flow Optimization & Bypass Logic:
   - Straight and Right-bound traffic inspects cars ahead via IsCarAhead(). 
   - Cars automatically bypass any left-turning car currently waiting inside the 
     designated Left-Turn Waiting Pocket, keeping overall traffic flowing smoothly.
